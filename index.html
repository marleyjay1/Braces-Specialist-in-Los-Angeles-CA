Who Can Benefit From Braces?

Braces are not limited to teenagers. Children, teens, and adults may all benefit from orthodontic treatment when teeth or bite alignment needs improvement.

Common reasons patients consider braces include:

  • Crowded or overlapping teeth
  • Crooked teeth
  • Gaps between teeth
  • Overbite
  • Underbite
  • Crossbite
  • Open bite
  • Difficulty maintaining proper oral hygiene because of tooth positioning
  • Concerns about the appearance of the smile

What Happens During a Braces Consultation?

The first step is usually an orthodontic consultation. During this appointment, the orthodontic provider examines the teeth, bite, and overall alignment. Diagnostic information may also be collected to help develop an appropriate treatment plan.

The specialist can then explain:

  1. Your current orthodontic concerns
  2. Whether braces are suitable for you
  3. Possible treatment options
  4. The expected treatment process
  5. How to care for your teeth during treatment
  6. What you can expect during follow-up appointments

Having these questions answered can make the orthodontic process easier to understand and help patients feel more comfortable about starting treatment.
